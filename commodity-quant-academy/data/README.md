# Synthetic datasets

Generated by `generate_data.py` (seed 2026). **Synthetic, not exchange data.** Levels, regimes and relationships are shaped to look like a base-metals desk. All counterparties are fictional.

| File | Rows | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| `lme_prices.csv` | 3 metals × business days 2022-01-03 → 2026-09-30 | `date, metal (zinc/copper/aluminium), cash, three_month, cash_3m_spread` (cash − 3M; > 0 = backwardation), `regime, stocks_t` |
| `forward_curves.csv` | last 12 month-ends × 3 metals × tenors 0–27 | `as_of, metal, tenor_months, price` |
| `fx_rates.csv` | business days | `date, usdinr` (INR per USD), `eurusd` (USD per EUR) |
| `freight_rates.csv` | weekly × 9 routes | `week_ending, route, mode, usd_per_t` (container lanes from Nhava Sheva/Chennai; Richards Bay→Krishnapatnam coal; Europe/Med spike Jan–Jun 2024) |
| `trade_book.csv` | ~850 trades, shipments 2025-01 → 2026-12 | `trade_id, trade_date, direction (S/P), counterparty, country, region, metal, grade, tonnes, shipment_month, bl_date, incoterm, load_port, destination, qp_type (M-1/M/M+1/fixed), qp_start, qp_end, premium_usd_t, provisional_price_usd_t` (cash on BL date), `provisional_pct, final_price_usd_t` (QP average of cash, blank if not priced), `invoice_currency, eurusd_at_bl, payment_terms, status` |
| `option_chains.csv` | as of 2026-09-30 | `as_of, underlying` (LME Zinc 3M / NIFTY synthetic), `underlying_price, expiry, t_years, strike, type (C/P), implied_vol, mid, bid, ask, open_interest, lot_size`. Prices from Black-76 on a smile with put skew. |
| `index_daily.csv` | business days | `date, open, high, low, close, india_vix_like` (synthetic index for options backtests) |

Sales run about 10–12 kt/month across 38 counterparties in Europe, the Middle East, North Africa, South Africa, SE Asia, LATAM and India. Purchases come from 3 suppliers on FCA terms.
